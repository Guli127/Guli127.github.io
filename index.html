03 — Current project在做的项目

Mentora — an AI teaching-loop agent一个 AI 教学闭环 Agent

Teachers photograph student work. The agent grades against the official mark scheme, diagnoses why the marks were lost, maps each error to a syllabus objective, generates targeted practice and flags at-risk students. The teacher reviews and releases with one click. 老师拍照上传作业，Agent 按官方 mark scheme 逐点批改、判断为什么失分、把每个错误挂到大纲知识点上、生成针对性练习并预警掉队学生。老师只在最后审核，一键下发。

Why it exists为什么做它

The problem问题

A subject teacher on 16 lessons a week loses hours to one loop: mark the paper → work out the error cause → map it back to the syllabus → find the right past-paper question for each student. The same mistake gets judged from scratch twenty times, and a struggling student is usually spotted two weeks too late. 一个每周 16 节课的学科老师，时间会被同一个循环反复吃掉：批改 → 分析错因 → 对回大纲 → 再给每个学生找一道合适的 past paper。同一个错误，在三十份作业里要重新判断二十次；而掉队的学生，通常要两周后才被发现。

Why existing tools don't close it现有工具为什么补不上

  • General chat assistants — one-shot, no memory, not aligned to any exam board.通用聊天助手——单次对话、无记忆、不对齐任何考试局标准。
  • AI tutors — built for the student side; they never touch the teacher's marking workflow.AI 学习助教——面向学生端，不进入教师的批改工作流。
  • Smart campus systems — administration and class management, not the inside of a subject lesson.智慧校园系统——面向行政与班级管理，不进入学科教学内部。
Three design principles三条设计原则
01

Natively aligned to the mark schemeMark scheme 原生对齐

Not “let the AI decide a score”. Every mark point is judged separately, and the model must point to the sentence in the student's answer that justifies it. Explaining how the decision was reached is what earns teacher trust (Nazaretsky et al., 2022). 不是「让 AI 看着办给个分」，而是逐个评分点判定，并且必须指出依据来自学生答案的哪一句。解释「AI 是怎么判的」正是提升教师信任的关键（Nazaretsky et al., 2022）。

02

A closed loop with long-term memory闭环 + 长期记忆

Not a chatbot that waits to be asked. Every student carries a mastery profile that updates each week, and the agent raises the warning itself — before the monthly exam, not after it. 不是等人提问的聊天机器人。每个学生有随周更新的知识点掌握画像，Agent 主动发起预警——在月考之前，而不是之后。

03

The teacher reviews; the teacher does not operate教师只审核，不操作

No prompts to write, no models to pick, no settings to configure. The human intervention point is review and release. Responsibility for a grade stays with a person — that one is not negotiable. 不用写提示词、不用选模型、不用配置参数。人的介入点在审核与下发。分数与评价的最终责任必须在人——这条不可让步。

Seven steps, one A-Level IT question. Click through them, or use the ← → arrow keys. 七个步骤，一道 A-Level IT 题目。逐步点击，或用键盘 ← → 切换。

What should we measure when a teacher marks with AI? 当老师用 AI 批改，我们究竟该测量什么？
